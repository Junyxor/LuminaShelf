# LuminaShelf brand mark

The v0.3 mark is intentionally flat and vector-first.

- Blue rounded field: keeps the visual neighborhood of digital-library tooling without copying a third-party wordmark.
- White `Z`: makes the client purpose legible at taskbar/tray size.
- Cyan open-book accent: differentiates LuminaShelf from a plain letter icon.
- No gradients, character art, stars, globe, tiny text, or decorative particles in the production icon.

`lumina-mark.svg` is the source of truth. Platform PNG/ICO/ICNS assets are generated from that SVG; do not hand-edit raster outputs.

Minimum-size rule: at 32 px the `Z` must remain a solid silhouette and the book accent may simplify visually, but must not merge into the bottom bar.
